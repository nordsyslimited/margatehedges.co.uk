Service · Hedge removal

Full removal, root and all, with the ground reinstated.

When the hedge has to go — for a new driveway, a wall, a fence, or because it is just wrong for the position. I take it out, grind the stumps, and leave the ground ready for whatever comes next.

What removal includes

  • Full above-ground removal, cleanly cut at ground level
  • Stump grinding where access allows — most Cliftonville and Northdown domestic plots fit a 26-inch grinder; some tight Old Town courtyards don't and need hand-clearing
  • Root-ball lift on smaller plants where they're worth saving for replanting
  • Topsoil reinstatement over the shallow chalk — Thanet topsoil is often under 15cm deep and the grinder leaves behind a mineral scatter that needs raking and levelling
  • All arisings removed from site to Manston Road HWRC or Richborough under my EA waste-carrier registration
  • CA tree-check first — six-week section-211 notice filed if you're inside one of the Margate CAs and any hedge tree is over 75mm at chest height

The Thanet chalk-and-sand reality

Removing a long-established hedge on Thanet Upper Chalk often surprises homeowners. Root systems lace sideways into the thin sand-cap topsoil rather than going down — you can have a 50-year-old privet hedge whose root mass is in the top 20cm of soil but extends 1.5m out from the line. Stump grinding picks up the main stems but the lateral root mass needs hand-clearing where you're planning to plant or build on the strip afterwards. On front-line Escallonia and Griselinia, the salt-hardened stems come out easier than you'd expect; on inland Leylandii, the buttress roots are the real work.

Conservation-area work

Removal triggers more careful CA checks than cutting or reduction. Hedging-species removal is allowed without a notice — but if there are any standards within the hedge (a holly, a mature hornbeam, an inherited yew) over the 75mm threshold, those need six-week notice or a TPO check. I do this before quoting, not on the day.

Listed-property boundary work: Margate Old Town has 2 Grade I, 10 Grade II* and 254 Grade II listed buildings, and boundary walls / gates / railings in a listed property's curtilage are also listed by default. Replacing a hedge with a wall — or removing a wall to plant a hedge — needs Listed Building Consent. I'll flag this at quote stage.

What I do with the material

Standard hedging-species clippings and small branches go to Manston Road HWRC (CT9 4LX, Mon–Sat 08:00–16:30, Sun 09:00–16:00, booking required) under my EA waste-carrier registration. Larger trunk wood from removed standards is chipped for green disposal. Nothing burned on site. HWRC doesn't accept commercial waste — that's why the licensed-carrier route matters and why untraceable "man with a van" removal is a bad idea.

Pricing

Removal pricing depends almost entirely on access. A 10m hedge with vehicle access for a chipper and grinder is straightforward — typically £600 – £1,200. The same hedge in a tight Cliftonville terrace front garden with everything wheelbarrow-out is materially more work. Fixed price, per job.
