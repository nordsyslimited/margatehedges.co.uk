### Carry-forwards from previous audits

**NR-2 — JSON-LD HTML entities in 7 articles (recurring)**

HTML-encoded ampersands (`&amp;`) and other entities inside `<script type="application/ld+json">` blocks break structured-data validators. The entities need to be literal characters inside JSON.

Files: `conifer-hedge-brown-patches-margate.html`, `fuchsia-magellanica-hedge-margate.html`, `hedge-nesting-season-margate.html`, `hedge-width-reduction-margate.html`, `hedgerow-management-regulations-2024-margate-kent.html`, `honey-fungus-hedge-margate.html`, `tamarisk-hedge-care-margate.html`.

**NR-3 — og:type missing on all 6 tree service pages (recurring)**

`services/tree/index.html`, `services/tree/pruning.html`, `services/tree/crown-thinning.html`, `services/tree/crown-raising.html`, `services/tree/crown-reduction.html`, `services/tree/deadwooding.html` — none carry `<meta property="og:type">`.

**NR-4 — privacy.html names NordSys Limited and Richard Lim (recurring)**

`privacy.html` contains: *"NordSys Limited, operated by Richard Lim"* and *"Richard Lim, NordSys Limited."* The PUBLIC-COPY RULE prohibits NordSys Limited on any public page. Privacy policies typically need a legal entity name — owner decision needed.

**NR-5 — `received-section-154-notice-what-to-do-margate.html` JSON-LD pattern (recurring)**

The article carries three JSON-LD blocks: Article + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage. Site convention for how-to articles is a flat Article block only. The BreadcrumbList and FAQPage additions are non-standard for this site. No functional harm, but inconsistent with the established pattern — owner decision.

**NR-6 — `services/hedge-planting.html` redirect destination (recurring)**

Redirects to `/services/tree/` rather than `/services/`. A visitor arriving for hedge planting lands on the tree-services page. Review whether the target is correct.
