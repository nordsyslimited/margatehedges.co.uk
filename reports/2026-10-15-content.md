## Topic-gap check

Reviewed all 65 existing how-to articles. Euonymus japonicus (Japanese spindle) was not covered by any existing slug. The site covers many coastal and salt-tolerant species but had no article on this widely-planted Cliftonville and Thanet front-garden hedge plant. Euonymus japonicus is genuinely distinct from other species already covered: its pruning calendar is strictly two-window (April and late August), it is among the most salt-tolerant evergreen hedging plants for the UK coast, and the specific frost-burn risk from October cuts is a recurring practical query that no existing article addresses. The article also introduces the distinction between the compact 'Microphyllus' cultivar and the standard large-leaved form, which matters for tool choice in narrow Cliftonville front gardens.
