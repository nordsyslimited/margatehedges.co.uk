### 2. NordSys Limited and Richard Lim in privacy.html (CARRY-FORWARD, HIGH)

`privacy.html` lines 27 and 55 name "NordSys Limited" and "Richard Lim" in the data-controller statement. AGENTS.md PUBLIC-COPY RULE (2026-10-05) says: never write NordSys or NordSys Limited on any public page; never write owner/operated-by statements; do not name the business owner by full name.
