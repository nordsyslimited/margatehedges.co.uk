## Auto-fixed

Although used in a physical-mechanics context (lever arm on an uprooted root plate), "leverage" is on the banned filler list in AGENTS.md without exception. Replaced with "the mechanical forces involved". The meaning is unchanged.

### 2. Double-hyphen em-dash substitutes — bay-laurel-laurus-nobilis-hedge-margate.html

The new bay-laurel article (shipped overnight) contained three instances of ` -- ` used as parenthetical em-dash substitutes:
- Line 36: "the leaves are large -- five to seven centimetres -- even a single bad cut"
- Line 75: "If an entire section -- a metre or more -- shows no new buds"
- Line 85: "Cherry laurel leaves are also longer -- ten to fifteen centimetres -- where"

AGENTS.md rule: "No em-dashes. Commas, full stops, colons or parentheses instead." All three replaced with parentheses. Example: `large (five to seven centimetres), even a single`.

Affected pages include the majority of how-to articles (bulk-edited on 12 October 2026), several service and area pages, the how-to index (updated today with the new bay-laurel card), and `about.html` / `privacy.html`.
