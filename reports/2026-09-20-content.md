**Gap identified:** Pleached hedges (hedge on stilts) had no article. Pleaching is a distinct training technique, producing a flat elevated canopy on a clear stem, not a ground-level hedge. It is the correct answer for a specific class of Margate and Cliftonville plot: narrow rear gardens where a first-floor extension or raised deck creates a sight-line problem above normal fence height, and where a conventional ground-level hedge would waste space or block light at the wrong level. The topic is genuinely uncovered (no slug containing "pleach" or "stilts" existed), genuinely local (small plots in Northdown, Cliftonville, Westbrook), and has a UK video source from an established authority channel.
