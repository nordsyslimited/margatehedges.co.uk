### 2. BreadcrumbList JSON-LD in received-section-154-notice article

`how-to/received-section-154-notice-what-to-do-margate.html` carries a standalone `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD block in addition to its flat `Article` block. The established site pattern for individual articles is a flat `Article` object only. The `@graph` + `BreadcrumbList` convention belongs to `how-to/index.html` (the hub), not to articles. The extraneous block should be removed.

### 3. formal-hedges-front-gardens-cliftonville.html — WCA 1981 and story-led opening

Two issues on this article:

**(a) Missing WCA 1981 citation.** The article discusses cut timing ("after nest check", "late May/early June after nest check") without naming the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 or its section 1 offence. Any article that touches cutting timing should cite the Act explicitly per TEMPLATES/how-to.md §4.

**(b) Structural-angle drift.** The lede reads: "Dalby Square, Ethelbert Road, the Norfolk-Warwick-Surrey block, Northdown Road: many streets in Cliftonville fall inside conservation areas..." This is a location/context opener, not the site's required job-story opening. It does not set up a specific-feeling scenario or named situation that the article's advice then resolves. Needs rewrite of the lede to lead with a concrete job anecdote before moving into the species comparison.

### 4. staged-hedge-reduction-margate-coastal.html — missing explicit WCA 1981 citation

`how-to/staged-hedge-reduction-margate-coastal.html` mentions the nesting window ("after the nesting window closes", "after nesting, before the equinoctial gales") but does not name the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 or its penalty (fine up to £5,000 per nest). The article is a timing-focused reduction guide and should cite the Act explicitly.
