## Topic gap check

Reviewed all 33 existing how-to slugs (including thuja-western-red-cedar, shipped 9 September). Cotoneaster was not covered. It is:
- The second most common hedging genus I encounter across CT8-CT9 gardens after Prunus laurocerasus, and the most likely to be mis-timed because "cotoneaster" covers multiple species with genuinely different cut windows
- Genuinely locally relevant: franchetii and lacteus are common on Westgate-on-Sea and Garlinge boundaries; simonsii appears on inter-war and postwar terrace fronts across Cliftonville and Northdown; horizontalis is standard on wall-trained front gardens from Palm Bay to the Old Town
- The berry-season/nesting-season timing conflict is a real practical problem I encounter several times a year -- it is precisely the kind of thing a Margate contractor guide should address
- C. horizontalis has a minor but genuine ecological note for SSSI-adjacent cliff-edge gardens (chalk grassland invasive), which is specifically Thanet-coast-relevant and not covered anywhere else on the site
