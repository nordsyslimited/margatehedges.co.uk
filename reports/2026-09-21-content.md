**Gap identified:** Hedge feeding and fertilising had no article. Feeding is a genuinely distinct maintenance topic (not covered by any species guide, any seasonal calendar, or any renovation article). It is particularly relevant for CT9 conditions: the free-draining chalk-over-gravel soils common in Westbrook, Garlinge, Palm Bay and coastal Cliftonville lose soluble nutrients faster than average UK loam, and the salt-laden coastal air pushes plants harder. The micronutrient lockout problem specific to high-pH chalk soils (interveinal chlorosis on griselinia, pittosporum and escallonia) is a distinctive local issue that many homeowners misread as a cutting or species problem. No existing article touched this. The topic maps clearly to the "Seasonality" hub category (feeding is principally a spring-window activity).
