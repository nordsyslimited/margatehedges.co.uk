## Topic-gap check

Reviewed all 63 existing how-to articles. Sea buckthorn (Hippophae rhamnoides) was not covered by any existing slug. The site already covers several native coastal and salt-tolerant species (rosa rugosa, salt-tolerant-hedges overview, griselinia, escallonia, olearia) but not sea buckthorn specifically. Sea buckthorn is genuinely distinct for three reasons that justify its own article: it is dioecious (male/female plants, sex ratio matters for berry crop), it spreads by underground stolons requiring annual sucker control, and it is naturalised on the Thanet coastline and dune grassland in a way that makes it a locally observable reference point for customers. The sucker-control angle is practically important and not covered anywhere else on the site.
