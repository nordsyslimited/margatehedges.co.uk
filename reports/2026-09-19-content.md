**Gap identified:** Traditional hedge laying had no article. Hedge laying is a distinct British countryside craft, widely covered on UK YouTube, that produces a fundamentally different outcome from trimming: it renovates a native hedgerow whose base has gone bare and which can no longer be fixed by annual trimming. This is a genuine separate topic from the staged-reduction article (which covers reducing the height/width of an overgrown hedge, not laying it) and from the bare-root planting article (which covers new planting, not renovation of existing stools). Relevant to rural-fringe CT8/CT9 properties in Garlinge, Northdown and the agricultural edge of Thanet.
