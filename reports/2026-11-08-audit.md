## Needs-review

Five open items (all persistent from previous audits):

### 1. privacy.html — NordSys Limited and Richard Lim (PUBLIC-COPY RULE violation)

`/privacy.html` lines 27 and 55 name "NordSys Limited" and "Richard Lim" twice. This violates the PUBLIC-COPY RULE (2026-10-05) which states: "Never write NordSys or NordSys Limited on any public page."

As a legal privacy/GDPR notice this page may need to identify the data controller for compliance. Judgment call required: either rewrite to remove the trading name and personal name, or confirm that GDPR compliance requires them here (in which case the PUBLIC-COPY RULE needs a documented exception for the privacy page contact block).

### 2. received-section-154-notice-what-to-do-margate.html — Non-standard JSON-LD

This article has three JSON-LD blocks: Article (flat, correct per convention), BreadcrumbList, and FAQPage. The BreadcrumbList is not part of the established Article JSON-LD pattern for individual how-to guides (the AGENTS.md says the index page uses CollectionPage + BreadcrumbList, not individual articles). The FAQPage adds value but may not match the site's established convention.

Decision required: remove BreadcrumbList from this article, or document this article as an intentional exception.

### 3. formal-hedges-front-gardens-cliftonville.html — Missing story-led opening

The article opens with a list of street names and a direct topic statement ("Dalby Square, Ethelbert Road, the Norfolk-Warwick-Surrey block, Northdown Road: many streets in Cliftonville fall inside conservation areas..."). This is a topic-first structure, not the required story-led opening.

House style (AGENTS.md, TEMPLATES/how-to.md): every how-to article opens with a short real-feeling job scenario before the practical detail. This article lacks the "A customer called me..." or "A common situation:" story hook. Content edit required.

### 4. staged-hedge-reduction-margate-coastal.html — WCA 1981 not cited by name

This article discusses cutting timing (mentions "nesting window closes", "after nesting", "Late August to mid-September, after nesting") and advises on cutting schedules around nesting. However, it never cites the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 by name, nor states the formal window dates (1 March to 31 August).

AGENTS.md requirement: "every article touching cutting/trimming timing must reference the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 nesting-season window (1 March–31 August)." Content edit required to add the statutory citation.
