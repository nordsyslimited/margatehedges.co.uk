## Needs review

### 1. privacy.html — NordSys Limited and Richard Lim (PERSISTENT)

`privacy.html` lines 27 and 55 contain:
- "Margate Hedges & Tree Services (trading name of NordSys Limited, operated by Richard Lim, Margate CT9)"
- "Richard Lim, NordSys Limited."

This breaches the PUBLIC-COPY RULE in AGENTS.md (no NordSys, no operated-by, no surname-plus-company). However, UK GDPR / UK DPA 2018 requires the data controller's legal name and contact details on a privacy policy. The wording may be legally required. Decision needed: either retain with a note that it is a legal obligation, or rephrase to satisfy both the rule and the legal requirement (e.g. omitting "NordSys Limited" and using only the contact email/phone as permitted identification).
