Hornbeam hedge care was a clear gap: Carpinus betulus is one of the most common garden hedging species in UK Victorian terrace gardens, widely planted in Cliftonville and Northdown, and had no dedicated article. The previous run (24 Aug) covered beech, which is the species most similar to hornbeam (both marcescent, both with late-August cut windows). The hornbeam article is deliberately distinct: different renovation characteristics (hornbeam regenerates from hard cuts into old wood; beech requires more caution), different coastal position (hornbeam is a reliable second-line species where beech can struggle on the salt-exposed front), and different tool considerations. The 24 Aug report flagged hornbeam as the recommended next topic.
